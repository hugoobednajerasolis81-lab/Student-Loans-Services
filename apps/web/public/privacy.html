Privacy policy

Information submitted through the website's inquiry and document-preparation forms is transmitted to the Student Loans Solutions application and stored in its configured customer-management database so an advisor can respond.

Do not submit your FSA ID, password, multi-factor authentication code, Social Security number, bank credentials or full student-loan account number through this website.

Access to advisor records is restricted to authorized workspace accounts. The site is an independent educational and inquiry service and is not affiliated with the U.S. Department of Education or a loan servicer.

For official federal student-loan information, visit StudentAid.gov.
